About StudyPlus

More Than Reading. A Lifestyle of Learning.

StudyPlus is a student-focused initiative committed to helping students become more intentional about their learning. We create opportunities for students to develop consistent reading habits, stay accountable, access practical learning resources, and grow academically and personally.

StudyPlus is more than a reading group. It is a student development initiative focused on helping students move from merely having academic goals to taking intentional action. Academic growth is not just about reading for examinations. It is also about developing sustainable learning habits, managing distractions, taking responsibility for personal development, and showing up consistently.

Our Mission

To help students develop consistent reading habits, become accountable for their learning, and build sustainable practices that support academic and personal growth.

Our Vision

To build a community of intentional learners who take ownership of their development and make continuous learning a lifestyle.

Reading Challenge

14-Day Reading Consistency Challenge

The StudyPlus 14-Day Reading Consistency Challenge is designed to help students build sustainable reading habits and become more intentional about their learning. It is open to students who want to become more consistent with their studies. The challenge begins with 14 days and can have recurring editions.

"This is not about unhealthy academic pressure, competition, or comparing reading hours. It is about building the habit of showing up consistently."

How It Works

  1. 1

    Set a realistic daily reading goal.

  2. 2

    Work towards that goal each day for 14 days.

  3. 3

    Share your progress through the designated accountability community check-in.

  4. 4

    Reflect on your learning experience and identify areas for improvement.

Challenge Guidelines

  • Commit to a realistic daily reading goal that fits your schedule.
  • Work towards your personal goal each day.
  • Share your progress through the designated accountability community check-in.
  • Your updates can include what you studied, your progress, your daily goal, or a brief reflection.
  • If you miss a day, do not give up. Resume your commitment.
  • Focus on consistency, personal progress, and accountability rather than comparing reading hours.
  • The challenge lasts for 14 days.
